Privacy Policy

Decision calculator is a tool for comparing options by rating pros and cons.

Using the app without signing in

The app can be used without signing in or saving data to application servers. When signed out, Save and Load download and read local .json files on the device.

Signing in with Google

When signing in with Google, only the Google account identifier and email address are requested. Profile pictures, contacts, and access to other Google services are not requested.

The account record, a session cookie, and any explicitly saved decision comparisons are stored in the application database.

Duplicate consideration detection

Clicking Detect duplicate considerations and confirming the prompt sends the text of the pros and cons to an external AI API solely to check for duplicate items.

Deleting data

Use Load → Delete to remove a saved comparison, or open the account menu and click Delete account to remove the account and all saved data from application servers.
