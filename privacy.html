Privacy Policy

Decision calculator is a tool for comparing options by rating pros and cons.

Using the app without signing in

Calculations run in your browser. When signed out, Save and Load only read and write .json files on your device.

Signing in with Google

If you choose to sign in with Google, we request only your Google account identifier and email address (openid email). We do not request your profile picture, contacts, or access to any other Google services.

We store your account record, an HTTP-only session cookie, and any decision comparisons you explicitly save in our database (Cloudflare D1).

Duplicate consideration detection

If you click Detect duplicate considerations and confirm the prompt, the text of your pros and cons is sent to an external AI API solely to check for duplicate items.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual saved comparisons from the Load dialog, or delete your account and all associated sessions and saved comparisons at any time from the account menu (Delete account).
